    \item[1.] Since $v_i$ are independent, the likelihood of $x$ and $\phi$ ($z_1, \dots, z_m$) is given by solving the following optimization problem:
    \[ \text{maximize} \quad \sum_{i=1}^n (z_i - A-I^T x)^2 \]
    According to the problem, we must have $\alpha \le \phi'(u) \le \beta$. Since the measurements are sorted ($y_i \le y_{i+1}$) and quantized, we get the inequality:
    \begin{align*}
      \alpha &\le \phi'(u) \le \beta \\
      \implies \alpha &\le \frac{y_{i+1} - y_i}{z_{i+1} - z_i} \le \beta &i = 1, \dots, m-1 \\
      \implies \beta &\le \frac{z_{i+1} - z_i}{y_{i+1} - y_i} \le \alpha &\text{(Since $\alpha, \beta > 0$)} \\
      \implies \beta (y_{i+1} - y_i) &\le z_{i+1} - z_i \le \alpha &(y_{i+1} - y_i) 
    \end{align*}
